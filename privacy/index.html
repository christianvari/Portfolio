Who is responsible

This site is run by Christian Vari. For any privacy question or request, write to info@codezen.tech.

What this site collects

There are no accounts, forms or newsletters. If you write to me, I use your email only to reply. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service.

With your consent, the site loads Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.) to measure visits and understand how pages are used. Clarity can record mouse movements, clicks and scrolling; text you type is masked. Ad features are disabled. These providers may transfer data to the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Nothing is loaded before you click “Accept”, and declining has no effect on how the site works.
